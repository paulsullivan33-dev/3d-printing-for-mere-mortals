# 3D Printing for Mere Mortals

Plain-language guides to 3D printing, written for people who want to
actually make things — especially on a printer at home.

No engineering degree required. No hype. Just what things are, why they
matter, and how to get a good print without the frustration.

## Start here

1. [How 3D Printing Works](how-3d-printing-works.md) — filament, hot
   ends, and layers, in plain words.
2. [Choosing a Printer](choosing-a-printer.md) — what actually matters
   when buying your first (or second) printer.
3. [Filament in Plain Language](filament-guide.md) — PLA, PETG, TPU,
   ABS/ASA: what each is good for.
4. [Your First Print](first-print.md) — unbox to finished object, with
   checks along the way.

## A note on language

These guides explain ideas simply. Simple wording is a choice, not a
judgment — the concepts underneath are the real ones, and the
[Glossary](glossary.md) gives you the proper terms so you can talk to
other printer owners without translation.

Every guide follows the same [writing criteria](criteria.md): plain
words, no hype, honest limits, and checkable steps.
